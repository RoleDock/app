# RoleDock — Product Vision

**Analyze. Apply. Track.**

## Problem

Candidates often spread their job-search information across job boards, browser
tabs, documents, email, notes, and spreadsheets.

When applying repeatedly, they need to:

- understand whether an offer fits their profile;
- identify what should be emphasized in their CV;
- prepare messages and cover letters;
- remember what was sent;
- track application progress;
- rapidly recover the context when a recruiter calls.

## Product

RoleDock is an intelligent workspace for preparing and tracking job
applications.

AI assists the workflow but is not the product itself. RoleDock should help the
candidate make better and faster decisions while keeping the candidate in
control.

## MVP v0.1

The MVP includes:

- a manually maintained candidate profile;
- pasted job offers;
- structured offer analysis;
- an explainable compatibility score;
- CV keywords;
- strengths and attention points;
- a generated short application message with a character limit;
- a generated cover letter;
- draft and application saving;
- application statuses;
- an application list;
- application details;
- a concise recruiter-call brief.

## Explicitly outside v0.1

- CV upload or parsing;
- LinkedIn or Indeed integration;
- scraping;
- Gmail integration;
- automatic follow-ups;
- automatic CV rewriting;
- autonomous agents;
- a mobile application.

A mobile client is a possible future client of the same backend API, not an MVP
requirement.

## Product principle

Validate usefulness before automating every friction. Do not build features
merely because they are technically possible.
